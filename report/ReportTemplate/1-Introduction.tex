\section{Introduction}
 
Formula one is a sport deeply grounded in research and development, that generates billions in revenue each year. With almost a billion international fans watching each race, it is a shame that the sport hasnt become more accessible. In addition, F1 teams are progressively becoming more data-driven and reliant within their strategy and development of their teams and cars. This paper proposes an approach that drives strategic insight via commentary suitable for the consumer and for the teams aiming to aquire assitence with data interpretation. From this paper consumers of Formula 1 with consumer level hardware should be able to access and use our solution, and teams may be able to find unseen insight promoting improved strategy.

Previous solutions and or proposed technology has not existed for this work, and most likely is soon to be considered with many of the Forumla teams creating strategic partnerships with world leaders in artificial intelligence. This paper aims to provide direction towards a valuable asset that may become standard within not only Formula 1 teams strategy, however addtionally adds to the existing courpus of research regarding autonomous sports commentary generation by incentivizing synthesized insight. We belive via a learned intelligent mapping from live telemetry and previous commentary, a fast consumer grade tool can provide useful intution.  

This paper illustrates the next step in Formula 1 commentary, and a different approach to other sports autonmous sports commentary generation. This is done with the model being built for synthesized insight, in lieu of providing a mere verbatim narration of the visual elements. Furthermore this approach is optimized to perform on consumer level hardware, ensuring accessiblity for consumers, and less powerful potentialy embedded applications.
