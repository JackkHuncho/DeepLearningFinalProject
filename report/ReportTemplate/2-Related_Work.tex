\paragraph{Automatic Sports Commentary Generation}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \cite{AutoBaseComm}

The research regarding intelligent autonomous sports commentary is extremely sparse, as often regarded within each source's related works. Formula one is not short of research; however, within this domain, the work is within infantancy. Our work, which involves applying such commentary to Formula 1, is a challenge only made possible via similar works done within baseball and basketball. The work undertaken by Lee on \cite{SCoReS} provides an insight on how we can make our commentary more insightful from an automated narrative. 
\paragraph{DPO vs. RLHF}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \cite{DPO}
    \item \cite{ouyang2022traininglanguagemodelsfollow}
    \item \cite{SFT}
\end{itemize}
A prime motivation within commentary been to provide listeners with insightful, most importantly entertaining dialouge. In previous contexts regarding large language models and the broader scope of natural language tasks, we have seen advancements in tailoring responses to match human preferences. A breakthrough within this realm has come from the explorations undergone by Ouyang \cite{ouyang2022traininglanguagemodelsfollow}. Although the benefits from introducing RLHF can greatly improve the commentary generated by an approach found by Rafailov \cite{DPO} would reap the benefits of such implementation without requiring as much overhead. Both are notable potentail additions upon our proposed methodology; however, with the added cost of compute or dataset additions, we are unable to implement such without risk of straying from the affordance of being consumer feasible and achievable within a month of our development. With more time, we would aim to implement DPO into our system.

\paragraph{Proper Dataset Creation}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \cite{AutoBaskData}
\end{itemize}
In contrast to exisiting datasets and achievments from similiar works such as Zhang \cite{AutoBaskData}, Formula 1 constantly makes regulation overhauls on average every 6-8 years. This creates an issue in dataset creation, as we are unable to use a significantly large set of previous years' telemetry data. Additionally, we were unable to ethically source visual data, which resulted in an absence of visual media within our dataset. In the creation of our dataset, we used strategies learned from Zhang to create a suitable dataset based upon the most up to date reglations within our targeted sport. We relied on historical telemetry data from OpenF1 and publicly available ground truth commentary.
