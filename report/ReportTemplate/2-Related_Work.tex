The research regarding intelligent autonmous sports commentary is extremely sparse as often regarded within each sources related works. Formula one is not short of research, however within this domain the work is within infantancy. Our work which invovles applying such commentary to Formula 1 is a challenge only made possible via simliar works done within baseball and basketball. \paragraph{DPO vs. RLHF}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \cite{DPO}
    \item \cite{ouyang2022traininglanguagemodelsfollow}
    \item \cite{SFT}
\end{itemize}
A prime motivation within commentary been to provide listeners with insightful, most importantly entertaining dialouge. In previous contexts regarding large langauge models and the broader scope of natural language tasks, we have seen advancements in tailoring responses to match human preference. A breakthrough within this realm has come from the explorations undergone by Ouyang \cite{ouyang2022traininglanguagemodelsfollow}. Although the benefits from introducing RLHF, can greatly improve the commentary generated an approach found by Rafailov \cite{DPO} would reap the benefits of such implemntation without requiring as much overhead. Both are notable potentail additions upon our proposed methodology, however with the added cost of compute or dataset additions we are unable to implement such without risk of straying from the affordance of being consumer feasible and achievable within a month.

\paragraph{Proper Dataset Creation}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \cite{AutoBaskData}
\end{itemize}
In contrast to exisiting datasets and achievments from similiar works such as Zhang \cite{AutoBaskData}, Formula 1 constantly makes regulation overhauls on average every 6-8 years. This creates an issue in dataset creation as we are unable to use a signifcantly large set of previous years telemetry data. Additionally we were unable to ethically source visual data which resulted in an absence of visual media within our dataset. In the creation of our dataset we used strategies learned from Zhang, to create a suitable dataset based upon the most up to date reglations within our targeted sport. We relied on historical telemetry data from OpenF1 and publicly available ground truth commentary.
